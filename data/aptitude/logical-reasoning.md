Direction Sense 
A man walks 5 km towards the South and then turns to the right. After walking 3 km, he turns to the left and walks 5 km. In which direction is he from the starting place?		(a) West (b) South (c) North-East (d) South-West					Answer - (d) South-West  Hint - Draw the path on graph paper, updating facing after each turn.

(a) 5 km North-East (b) 7 km East (c) 5 km West (d) 7 km West				Answer - (a) 5 km North-East  Hint - Track net north-south and east-west displacement, then use Pythagoras.
