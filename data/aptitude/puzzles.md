4 people cross a bridge at night with a torch. Bridge holds max 2 people. Speeds are 1, 2, 5, 10 mins. Minimum time to cross?							(a) 19 mins (b) 21 mins (c) 17 mins (d) 18 mins					Answer - (c) 17 mins  Hint - Send the two slowest together; let the fastest shuttle the torch and compare that strategy against ferrying everyone individually.
